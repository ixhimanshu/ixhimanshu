## 🧩 Product Universe by Morbid Technologies

### 1) 🔮 Morbid
**Website:** [morbid.app](https://morbid.app/)  
**Web App:** [app.morbid.app](https://app.morbid.app/)  
**Google Play:** [Morbid: Talk & Feel Better](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.morbid_tech.morbid)

- Emotional support platform for people who want a private space to talk without judgment.
- Connects users with real listeners through chat, voice call, or video call.
- Built around a pay-per-minute model instead of subscriptions.
- Designed for real-time support around stress, loneliness, heartbreak, overthinking, and everyday emotional overload.

---

### 2) 💬 Loof App
**Website:** [loof.app](https://www.loof.app/)  
**Google Play:** [LOOF: Make Friend Online](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.loof.loof)

- Anonymous emotional support and voice-chat platform focused on private conversations.
- Lets users talk to listeners without revealing personal identity or using real photos.
- Highlights instant matching, 24/7 availability, safety moderation, and support for multiple languages.
- Built for users who want to express feelings freely, especially around stress, loneliness, or heartbreak.

---

### 3) 🧘 Tatva
**Project Page:** [morbidtechnologies.com/projects/tatva](https://morbidtechnologies.com/projects/tatva/)  
**Google Play:** [Tatva: AI Astrology & Wellness](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tatva.tatva)

- AI companion app built around three distinct saathis: Jyoti for Vedic astrology, Riya for emotional companionship, and Dr. Meera for wellness guidance.
- Combines Kundli, Rashifal, Panchang, compatibility tools, supportive conversations, and everyday health guidance in one experience.
- Differentiated by shared memory across companions, so user context carries between sessions instead of starting from zero every time.
- Designed for an Indian audience with culture-aware, Hinglish-first interactions and clear safety boundaries around astrology, wellness, and crisis support.
- Includes a free tier with Tatva Pro for extended usage, deeper memory continuity, and full access to premium features.

---

### 4) 🛠️ TuckTools
**Website:** [tucktools.com](https://www.tucktools.com/)

- 100% free web toolkit for social media, creator workflows, website utilities, and developer tasks.
- Includes follower counters, hashtag generators, downloaders, screenshot tools, and profile or bio helpers.
- Covers Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook, Pinterest, and other creator-focused use cases.
- Positioned as a fast, lightweight, privacy-friendly tools hub rather than a mobile app.

---

### 5) 🌾 AgriRate
**Website:** [agrirate.com](https://agrirate.com/)

- Mandi price and supply intelligence platform for agricultural markets across India.
- Lets users explore commodity, state, district, and market-level data with structured filters.
- Focuses on average prices, arrivals and supply, historical trends, and downloadable CSVs for deeper analysis.
- Built for growers, buyers, traders, and agri teams making pricing, procurement, dispatch, and storage decisions.
